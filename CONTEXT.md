# myPeedika

myPeedika builds and maintains Shopify storefronts for small and medium businesses in India and the Gulf, and sells its own Shopify-connected software. The site is a single marketing page plus a blog.

## Language

### What we sell

**Service**:
Work performed for a paying client on their own Shopify store. There are exactly four, and they are the four cards in the services grid.
_Avoid_: offering, solution, package

**Store**:
A Shopify storefront belonging to a client. Never our own site.
_Avoid_: site, shop, website

**Work**:
A completed client store shown publicly on the site, with the client's real name and a link to the live storefront. A Work always points at a real, reachable store.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, case study, project, showcase

**App**:
Shopify-connected software myPeedika owns and sells under its own name. There are two: Replyr and Shopalizer. An App is a product with its own users, not work done for a client.
_Avoid_: product, tool, SaaS, platform

**App work**:
Shopify apps installed, configured, or custom-built for a client. This is a Service, not an App — the distinction is ownership. App work appears in the services grid; Apps appear in their own section.

### The apps

**Replyr**:
Our live app. AI Instagram DM automation that answers shopper questions using live Shopify product and order data. Its domain is dmreplyr.app but the product is named Replyr.
_Avoid_: dmreplyr, DM Replyr

**Shopalizer**:
Our unreleased Chrome extension. Reveals which theme and which apps any Shopify store is running — Wappalyzer for Shopify.

**Early access**:
The pre-launch state of an App that has no public release yet. Currently only Shopalizer. An Early access App shows a signup, never a purchase or install path.
_Avoid_: coming soon, beta, waitlist

### Design vocabulary

**Paper**:
The warm off-white page ground, `#fcf8f5`. The default background of the site and of every card.
_Avoid_: white, background, cream

**Ink**:
The near-black used for all text, `#212121`. Also the fill of inverted cards.
_Avoid_: black, foreground

**Accent**:
Teal `#15a89a` or brand green `#2b7150`. An Accent is only ever a fill or decorative shape, never text. Teal carries Ink text; the darker brand green carries Paper text.

**Product palette**:
The colours belonging to one of our Apps rather than to myPeedika — Replyr's indigo, Shopalizer's purple. A Product palette appears only inside that App's own card, never anywhere else on the site.

**Inverted card**:
A card filled with Ink instead of Paper, used sparingly to break up a grid. Exactly one per services grid, and one in the closing call to action. Nowhere else.
