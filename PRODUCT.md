# myPeedika — Product Context

## Product Purpose
myPeedika is a Shopify implementation service for Indian small and medium businesses. We build, design, fix, and maintain Shopify stores. We are not a SaaS product — we are a service company. Our clients are non-technical Indian business owners who sell products (clothing, baby products, food, jewellery, home goods) and need a professional online store without handling the technical side themselves.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Primary: Indian small business owners, age 25–50, non-technical, often selling via Instagram/WhatsApp already, want to go online properly
- Secondary: Growing Indian businesses that have a broken or outdated Shopify store
- Language: English site but users are Indian — they think in INR, expect UPI/Razorpay, relate to festival sales, WhatsApp communication
- They are NOT developers or designers. They judge a website on: "Does it look trustworthy? Can I understand it quickly? Can I reach these people on WhatsApp?"

## Brand Tone
- Warm, direct, human. Like talking to a knowledgeable friend, not a corporation.
- Plain English, zero jargon. Never use "AI-powered" or "platform" or "leverage".
- Confident without being loud.
- Indian without being stereotypical.

## Anti-references
- DO NOT look like a generic Tailwind UI template dump
- DO NOT use generic stock illustration shadows or clip-art icons
- DO NOT use blue as a primary color (it's too generic)
- DO NOT use the word "solutions" anywhere
- DO NOT make it look like a SaaS product — we are a service company

## Strategic principles
- Trust first: real client names, real results, real process
- Human before technical: show people, not just UI mockups
- Indian context always: INR pricing, Indian payment logos, WhatsApp CTA
